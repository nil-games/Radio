Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: seamless tileable floor material texture for a video game

Create a perfectly seamless full-frame square texture of old parquet flooring typical of a tidy ordinary Russian / post-Soviet apartment from the 1990s–2000s. Quiet cohesive floor surface with an even overall value when viewed from a distance.

The parquet is old but cared for and structurally intact. Age is communicated subtly through a gently dulled satin varnish, softened wood grain and very slight low-contrast variation, not through damage. No dirt, dust, stains, scratches, gouges, cracks, chips, gaps, missing blocks, lifted boards, water damage, mold, blackened seams, paint, debris or heavy wear.

Visual treatment compatible with the existing stylized comic-book apartment assets: restrained hand-painted wood grain, delicate brush texture and subtle 2–3 step color variation. Do not draw bold black outlines around every plank. Joints should be thin, soft dark-brown lines rather than graphic black ink. Material must remain readable as wood while functioning as a calm background floor surface.

The parquet surface fills every pixel from corner to corner. Perfectly flat orthographic top-down texture capture, uniform scale, no perspective, no camera tilt, no room, no walls, no baseboards, no furniture, no objects. Uniform neutral illumination with no baked directional light, shadows, highlights, reflections, ambient occlusion, gradients or vignette.

The herringbone pattern, joints, wood grain and color must continue naturally across the left/right and top/bottom boundaries with no broken blocks, cut-pattern mismatch, border, seam, edge darkening or central focal point.
